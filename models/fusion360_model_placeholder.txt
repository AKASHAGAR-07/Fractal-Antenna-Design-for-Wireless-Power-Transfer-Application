================================================================================
Autodesk Fusion 360 Sketch Coordinates - WPT Fractal Antenna
================================================================================

This document contains the exact coordinates for the 2D sketch profiles to draw 
the patch and slots in Autodesk Fusion 360.

1. SKETCH PLANE: XY Plane
   - Center Origin: (X=0.00, Y=25.00) mm
   - Note: The substrate starts at Y=0.00 and extends to Y=49.41.
     The patch center is placed at Y=25.00 to keep it centered on the substrate.

2. RADIATING PATCH OUTER BOUNDARY (Iteration 0)
   - Polygon Type: Circumscribed
   - Center: (0.00, 25.00) mm
   - Outer Vertex Radius (Side length e): 17.39 mm
   - Vertices:
     * V1: (17.39, 25.00)
     * V2: (8.70, 40.06)
     * V3: (-8.70, 40.06)
     * V4: (-17.39, 25.00)
     * V5: (-8.70, 9.94)
     * V6: (8.70, 9.94)

3. CENTRAL HEXAGONAL SLOT (Iteration 1)
   - Polygon Type: Circumscribed
   - Center: (0.00, 25.00) mm
   - Radius: 5.80 mm (1/3 of outer radius)
   - Vertices:
     * S1: (5.80, 25.00)
     * S2: (2.90, 30.02)
     * S3: (-2.90, 30.02)
     * S4: (-5.80, 25.00)
     * S5: (-2.90, 19.98)
     * S6: (2.90, 19.98)

4. SECONDARY HEXAGONAL SLOTS (Iteration 2)
   - Polygon Type: Circumscribed
   - Radius: 1.93 mm (1/9 of outer radius)
   - Slots are centered on the vertices of an intermediate hexagon of radius 8.70 mm:
     * Slot 2A Center: (8.70, 25.00)
     * Slot 2B Center: (4.35, 32.53)
     * Slot 2C Center: (-4.35, 32.53)
     * Slot 2D Center: (-8.70, 25.00)
     * Slot 2E Center: (-4.35, 17.47)
     * Slot 2F Center: (4.35, 17.47)

5. MICROSTRIP FEEDLINE
   - Rectangle (2-Point)
   - Corner 1: (-1.50, 0.00) mm
   - Corner 2: (1.50, 6.64) mm
   - This creates a feedline of Width = 3.00 mm (c) and Length = 6.64 mm (d),
     connecting from the edge of the substrate directly to the bottom edge
     of the hexagonal patch.

================================================================================
EXPORT INSTRUCTIONS:
- Click the sketch node in the Browser hierarchy, right-click, and select "Save As DXF" for import.
- Or Extrude the profile (patch - slots) by 0.05 mm (direction: New Body).
- Extrude feedline by 0.05 mm (Join).
- Go to File -> Export, select STEP (.step) or IGES (.iges) and save to your desktop.
